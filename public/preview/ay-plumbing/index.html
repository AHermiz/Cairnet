Where your water comes in

Water filtration

We'll help you choose and install a filtration system that suits your home, whether that's a tap on your kitchen counter, the water line to your fridge, or every drop that enters the house.

  • Reverse osmosis systems for a kitchen tap or fridge
  • Whole-house (point-of-entry) systems
  • Water softeners for hard water
  • Supplying filters for your home
  • Installing a system you already own
  • Routine filter replacement

What's in a typical under-sink reverse osmosis system

  1. Sediment prefilter: reduces particles such as rust and sand.
  2. Carbon prefilter: can reduce chlorine, taste and odour.
  3. RO membrane: reduces the dissolved substances the maker lists.
  4. Postfilter: can improve taste before water reaches the tap.
  5. Optional remineralization: adds minerals back for taste.

Results depend on the system and its certified performance. Check each product's specifications for what it addresses.
